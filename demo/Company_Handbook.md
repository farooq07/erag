# Acme Corp — Employee Handbook 2024

**Version:** 8.1 | **Effective Date:** 1 January 2024 | **Classification:** Internal

## Welcome

Acme Corp is a technology company headquartered in London, UK, with offices in Amsterdam, New York, and Singapore. Founded in 2011, we have grown to over 340 employees. Our mission: help organisations unlock the value of their data through intelligent software.

## 1. Our Values

- **Curiosity** — Ask questions, explore ideas, never stop learning
- **Ownership** — Take responsibility for our work and our customers' success
- **Candour** — Communicate openly and honestly
- **Inclusion** — Build an environment where everyone can do their best work
- **Impact** — Focus on outcomes, not activity

## 2. Code of Conduct

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect
- Avoid conflicts of interest; disclose any potential conflicts to HR
- Do not accept gifts or hospitality worth more than **£50** without prior written approval
- Report suspected misconduct via the Ethics Hotline: **ethics@acmecorp.com** or **0800 123 4567** (anonymous)

## 3. Benefits

- **Pension:** Acme Corp contributes **6% of base salary**; employee must contribute at least **3%**
- **Private Health Insurance:** Bupa, from day one; family cover at subsidised rate (employee pays 30%)
- **L&D Budget:** **£1,500 per employee per year** for courses, conferences, books, certifications
- **Mental Health:** **6 free counselling sessions/year** via EAP (eap.acmecorp.com or 0800 456 7890)
- **Hybrid Working:** Minimum **2 days in office per week**; fully remote requires Director approval
- **Home Office Allowance:** **£300** upon joining for equipment

## 4. Performance Reviews

- **Mid-year review** (July): progress check-in
- **Year-end review** (December): formal rating, salary review, bonus determination
- Scale: Exceptional / Exceeds / Meets / Developing / Below Expectations

## 5. Compensation

- Annual salary reviews effective **1 February**
- Bonus: up to **15%** of base salary (20% Senior/Lead, 25% Director+), paid in March
- ESOP: available for Grade 6+, **4-year vesting** with **1-year cliff**

## 6. Data Protection

All employees must complete annual GDPR training. Suspected data breaches must be reported to the DPO within **1 hour** at **dpo@acmecorp.com**.
