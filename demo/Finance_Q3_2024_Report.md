# Acme Corp — Q3 2024 Financial Report

**Period:** 1 July – 30 September 2024 | **Department:** Finance | **Classification:** Internal Confidential

## Executive Summary

Acme Corp delivered strong Q3 2024 results with total revenue of **£18.4 million**, a **14% increase** over Q2 2024 (£16.1 million) and a **22% increase** year-over-year vs Q3 2023 (£15.1 million). EBITDA reached **£4.2 million** (22.8% margin), up from £3.5 million in Q2 2024.

## Revenue Breakdown

| Division | Q3 2024 (£M) | Q2 2024 (£M) | YoY Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| SaaS Subscriptions | 9.2 | 7.8 | +31.4% |
| Professional Services | 5.6 | 5.1 | +14.3% |
| Hardware & Licensing | 2.4 | 2.2 | +14.3% |
| Maintenance & Support | 1.2 | 1.0 | +9.1% |
| **Total Revenue** | **18.4** | **16.1** | **+21.9%** |

## Profit & Loss Summary

| Item | Q3 2024 (£M) | Q2 2024 (£M) |
|---|---|---|
| Gross Profit | 12.2 | 10.4 |
| EBITDA | 4.2 | 3.5 |
| Net Profit | 2.4 | 1.9 |
| Net Profit Margin | 13.0% | 11.8% |

## Key Performance Indicators

- Monthly Recurring Revenue: **£3.07M** (Q2: £2.60M)
- Annual Recurring Revenue: **£36.8M**
- Net Revenue Retention: **118%**
- Customer Acquisition Cost: **£4,200** (down from £4,800 in Q2)

## Cash Flow

- Opening cash balance (1 July 2024): **£8.3 million**
- Closing cash balance (30 September 2024): **£11.3 million**
- Revolving credit facility (Barclays, £5M): **£0 drawn**

## Headcount

Total headcount: **347 employees** (Q2: 321). 26 new hires in Sales (10), Engineering (9), Customer Success (7).

## Q4 2024 Outlook

Projected Q4 revenue: **£19.5–20.5 million**. Qualified pipeline: **£14.2 million**. CapEx budget: **£1.8 million** (data centre expansion).
