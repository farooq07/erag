# Acme Corp — Operations Standard Operating Procedures

**Version:** 5.0 | **Effective Date:** 1 April 2024 | **Owner:** Operations

## 1. Vendor Onboarding

New vendor onboarding steps:
1. Submit New Vendor Request via procurement portal
2. Operations due diligence within **5 working days**
3. Legal review of vendor terms within **5 working days**
4. Finance sets up payment terms
5. MSA required if annual value exceeds **£25,000**
6. Vendor activated in ERP (SAP)

**Typical onboarding time: 10–15 working days**

## 2. Procurement Thresholds

| Amount | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Up to £500 | Line Manager |
| £501 – £5,000 | Department Head |
| £5,001 – £25,000 | Director + Finance |
| £25,001 – £100,000 | CFO |
| Over £100,000 | CFO + CEO |

All purchases over **£2,000** require a formal Purchase Order in SAP before commitment. Emergency procurement up to £10,000 requires a retrospective PO within **24 hours**.

## 3. Office and Facilities

- Office hours: **Monday–Friday, 08:00–20:00**
- Hot-desking policy: all employees; book via Desk Booking app up to **2 weeks in advance**
- Meeting rooms: auto-released if unused **15 minutes** after booking start
- External visitors: pre-register **24 hours in advance**, sign in at reception, escorted at all times

## 4. Health & Safety

**Fire evacuation:** Leave via nearest fire exit, do not use lifts, assemble at front car park muster point. **First aid:** See notice boards; first-aid kit near kitchen on each floor. **Accident reporting:** Report to Operations within **24 hours** using the Incident Report form.

## 5. Business Continuity

- Recovery Time Objective (RTO): **4 hours** for critical systems
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO): **1 hour** for financial data; **4 hours** for general data

## 6. Environmental Commitments

- Net-zero carbon by **2035**
- 100% renewable electricity across all UK offices (achieved 2023)
- Company fleet: 100% EV by **2026**
- Single-use plastics eliminated from all offices since January 2024
