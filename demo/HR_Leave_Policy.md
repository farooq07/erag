# Acme Corp — HR Leave Policy 2024

**Effective Date:** 1 January 2024 | **Department:** HR | **Version:** 3.2

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement

All full-time permanent employees are entitled to **25 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, accruing at 2.08 days per month. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on contracted hours.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to **10 days of paid sick leave** per year without a medical certificate. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a doctor's certificate must be provided to HR within 5 working days. Extended sick leave beyond 10 days may be approved at 50% of base salary for up to 90 days.

## 3. Parental Leave

### Maternity Leave
Employees with at least 12 months continuous service are entitled to **20 weeks of fully paid maternity leave**, followed by an optional **10 weeks of unpaid leave**. To apply, notify HR in writing at least 8 weeks before the expected due date, and submit Form HR-09.

### Paternity Leave
Eligible employees are entitled to **4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave**, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption date.

## 4. Bereavement Leave

- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): **5 working days**
- Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle): **3 working days**
- Close friend: **1 working day**, subject to manager approval

## 5. Carry-Over Policy

Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** or they are forfeited. Directors and above may carry over up to 10 days with CEO approval.

## 6. Leave Request Process

Submit via HR portal at least **5 working days** in advance for short leave, and **4 weeks** for leave exceeding 5 days. The line manager reviews within 3 working days.

## 7. Working Hours and Overtime

Standard working hours are **09:00–17:30, Monday to Friday** (37.5 hours/week). Overtime is voluntary and pre-approved: **1.5× hourly rate** for the first 10 hours/month, **2× hourly rate** thereafter.
