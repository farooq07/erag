# Acme Corp — Travel and Expenses Policy

**Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 1 February 2024 | **Owner:** Finance

## 1. Booking Travel

All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal (**TravelPerk**) at least **14 days** before departure where possible. Bookings made fewer than 3 days before travel need Department Head approval.

- Flights under **6 hours**: economy class
- Flights over **6 hours**: premium economy; business class requires CFO approval
- Rail: standard class; first class allowed on journeys over **3 hours**

## 2. Hotels

Maximum nightly rates (including taxes):
- London: **£220**
- New York: **$350**
- Amsterdam: **€230**
- Singapore: **S$380**
- Elsewhere: **£150**

## 3. Meals and Per Diems

- Daily meal allowance while travelling: **£60** (UK), **£75** (international)
- Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events
- Client entertainment: up to **£80 per head**, pre-approved by a Director

## 4. Mileage

Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at **45p per mile** for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year and **25p per mile** thereafter. Commuting to your normal office is not reimbursable.

## 5. Submitting Expenses

Submit claims in **Expensify** within **30 days** of the expense, with itemised receipts for anything over **£25**. Claims older than **90 days** will not be reimbursed. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll.

## 6. Corporate Cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than **6 times a year**. Personal use of a corporate card is prohibited; any personal charge must be repaid within **7 days**.
