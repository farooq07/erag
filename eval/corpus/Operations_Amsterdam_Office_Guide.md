# Acme Corp — Amsterdam Office Guide

**Version:** 1.1 | **Effective Date:** 1 June 2024 | **Owner:** Operations

## 1. Location and Access

The Amsterdam office is at **Herengracht 182**, 3rd floor. Building access uses your Acme badge; the front door is open **07:30–19:00, Monday to Friday**. Weekend access must be requested from Facilities **48 hours** in advance.

## 2. Desks and Rooms

- The office has **40 desks** and 4 meeting rooms
- Desks are booked through the same Desk Booking app as London, up to **1 week** in advance
- The large meeting room "Prinsengracht" seats **12** and has video conferencing

## 3. Getting There

The nearest tram stop is **Dam** (lines 2, 12, 13, 17). Bicycle parking is in the basement; there is no car parking. Public transport costs for commuting are reimbursed up to **€150 per month**.

## 4. Local Holidays

Amsterdam staff follow Dutch public holidays, including **King's Day (27 April)** and Liberation Day (5 May, every five years).

## 5. Emergencies

The fire assembly point is the bridge across Herengracht to the left of the entrance. Emergency number in the Netherlands: **112**. The office first-aider is listed at reception.
