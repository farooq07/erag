# Acme Corp — Data Protection Policy

**Version:** 3.0 | **Effective Date:** 25 May 2024 | **Owner:** Legal / Data Protection Officer

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Acme Corp complies with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 when processing personal data of employees, customers and suppliers.

## 2. Data Protection Officer

The DPO is **Priya Nair**, reachable at **dpo@acmecorp.com**.

## 3. Lawful Basis

Every processing activity must have a documented lawful basis recorded in the Record of Processing Activities (ROPA), which Legal reviews every **6 months**.

## 4. Data Subject Requests

Requests to access, correct or delete personal data (DSARs) must be forwarded to the DPO the same day. Acme must respond within **one calendar month**; this can be extended by two further months for complex requests.

## 5. Personal Data Breaches

Any suspected breach must be reported to the DPO immediately. If the breach is likely to put individuals at risk, the DPO notifies the ICO within **72 hours** of Acme becoming aware of it.

## 6. Retention Periods

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Employee HR files | **6 years** after employment ends |
| Unsuccessful candidate data | **6 months** after the decision |
| Customer contracts | **7 years** after expiry |
| CCTV footage | **30 days** |

## 7. International Transfers

Personal data may only leave the UK under an approved transfer mechanism (adequacy decision or the International Data Transfer Agreement).

## 8. Training

All staff complete data protection training at induction and **annually**.
