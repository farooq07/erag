# Acme Corp — Recruitment and Onboarding Guide

**Version:** 1.6 | **Effective Date:** 1 January 2024 | **Department:** HR

## 1. Opening a Role

Hiring managers raise a **Headcount Request (Form HR-02)** in Workday. New roles need Department Head and Finance approval; backfills need Department Head approval only.

## 2. Interview Process

1. Recruiter screen (30 minutes)
2. Hiring manager interview
3. Skills assessment or technical interview
4. Values interview with someone outside the team

Feedback must be submitted in Workday within **48 hours** of each interview. Offers are approved by HR and the Department Head.

## 3. Referral Bonus

Employees who refer a successful hire receive a **£2,000** referral bonus, paid after the new joiner completes **6 months** of service. Referrals for Director-level roles earn **£4,000**.

## 4. Probation

All new employees complete a **6-month probation period**. A probation review meeting is held at **3 months** and at **6 months**. Probation may be extended once by up to **3 months**.

## 5. Notice Periods

| Situation | Notice |
|---|---|
| During probation | **1 week** either side |
| Grades 1–5 after probation | **1 month** |
| Grades 6–7 | **2 months** |
| Director and above | **3 months** |

## 6. First Day

New joiners receive their laptop on day one from the IT Helpdesk and attend an induction session at **10:00** in the London office or online. Mandatory training (GDPR, security awareness, code of conduct) must be completed within the first **14 days**.

## 7. Buddy Programme

Every new joiner is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for their first **90 days**.
