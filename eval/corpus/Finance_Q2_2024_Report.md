# Acme Corp — Q2 2024 Financial Report

**Period:** 1 April – 30 June 2024 | **Department:** Finance | **Classification:** Internal Confidential

## Executive Summary

Q2 2024 total revenue was **£16.1 million**, up **9%** on Q1 2024 (£14.8 million). EBITDA was **£3.5 million** (21.7% margin). Growth was led by SaaS subscriptions; hardware sales were flat after a large Q1 public-sector order.

## Revenue Breakdown

| Division | Q2 2024 (£M) | Q1 2024 (£M) |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS Subscriptions | 7.8 | 7.1 |
| Professional Services | 5.1 | 4.6 |
| Hardware & Licensing | 2.2 | 2.3 |
| Maintenance & Support | 1.0 | 0.8 |
| **Total Revenue** | **16.1** | **14.8** |

## Profit & Loss Summary

- Gross Profit: **£10.4 million**
- Net Profit: **£1.9 million** (11.8% margin)
- Operating expenses rose **6%**, mainly Engineering hiring

## Key Performance Indicators

- Monthly Recurring Revenue: **£2.60M**
- Customer Acquisition Cost: **£4,800**
- Gross churn: **1.9%** per quarter
- Average contract length: **26 months**

## Cash Flow

- Opening cash balance (1 April 2024): **£7.1 million**
- Closing cash balance (30 June 2024): **£8.3 million**

## Headcount

Total headcount: **321 employees** (Q1: 304).

## Risks Flagged

- Two enterprise renewals worth **£1.2 million** combined slipped to Q3
- FX exposure on USD contracts; hedging policy to be reviewed by the CFO in Q3
