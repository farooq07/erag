# Acme Corp — IT Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.3 | **Effective Date:** 1 March 2024 | **Owner:** IT

## 1. Scope

This policy covers all company devices, accounts, networks and software, and any personal device used to access company data.

## 2. Company Devices

- Laptops are issued with full-disk encryption (BitLocker or FileVault) enabled
- Devices lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity
- Laptops are refreshed every **3 years**
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Helpdesk within **2 hours**

## 3. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal phones may access email and chat only after enrolling in **Microsoft Intune**. Personal laptops may not be used to access Confidential or Restricted data. IT may remotely wipe the company container on an enrolled personal device.

## 4. Email and Messaging

- Company email must not be auto-forwarded to personal accounts
- External attachments over **25 MB** must be shared via OneDrive links
- Suspicious emails should be reported with the **Report Phish** button in Outlook

## 5. Internet Use

Reasonable personal use is allowed outside working hours. Streaming, torrenting and gambling sites are blocked on the corporate network.

## 6. Generative AI Tools

Only approved AI tools (currently **DocuMind** and **Microsoft Copilot**) may be used with Internal or Confidential data. Never paste Restricted data into any AI tool.

## 7. Software Installation

Employees may install software from the Company Portal without approval. Anything else requires a license request through the IT Service Desk.

## 8. Monitoring

Company systems are monitored for security purposes in line with UK law. Logs are retained for **12 months**.
